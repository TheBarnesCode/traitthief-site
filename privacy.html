Trait Thief: Privacy Policy

Published by The Barnes Code. Last updated 2026-11-21. Applies to Trait Thief version 1.0.0.

In short

Trait Thief does not collect any data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress (which levels you have solved and your stars) and your settings (sound, music, haptics, motion and Night mode) on your device only. This information is never sent anywhere and we cannot see it.

Network and third parties

The app makes no network requests and works fully offline. It uses no third-party services, no advertising identifier and no tracking of any kind. It asks for no permissions: no camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or notifications.

Children

Because the app does not collect data from anyone, it does not collect data from children either.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings and choose Restart progress to erase your stars and progress. Deleting the app from your device removes all of its saved data.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
