Trait Thief: Support

Version 1.0.0, published by The Barnes Code.

How to play

  1. Every object has a trait: Float, Heavy, Sticky or Spin. Tap one object, then another, to swap their traits.
  2. You have a limited number of swaps. Undo and Reset let you take swaps back and plan again.
  3. Press Play and watch the machine run. The same swaps always give the same result.
  4. If the goal is not met, the game tells you what went wrong. Swap again and try another plan.

Starting again

In the app, open Settings and choose Restart progress to erase your stars and progress.

Restoring your progress

Progress is saved only on your device. It is not stored on a server, so it cannot be restored on another device, and deleting the app deletes it.

Something wrong?

Tell us which level (for example 2-7), what you did and what you expected, and which iPhone and iOS version you use.
